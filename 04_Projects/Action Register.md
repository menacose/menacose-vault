## Use

A specific next step starts with a verb. Related work identifies its project, activity or area; use the links above to open the note. Add new choices through the column menu as work grows.

Backlog = uncommitted; Pending = accepted and awaiting start; In Review = awaiting review; Blocked = help/dependency needed; Deferred = postponed; Cancelled = no longer needed. Dates stay blank until agreed.

Initial actions and their existing owners, dates, statuses and notes were carried forward from [[Meeting Agenda and MoMs 20261018_Update]]. Use [[Work Overview]] for the status of the whole project or activity.
