# Secretary Transition And Volunteer Vacancy Announcement

## LinkedIn

**A Heartfelt Thank You to Hassan Mohamed | INCOSE MENA Chapter Secretary**

As we continue to grow the INCOSE MENA Chapter, we would like to take a moment to express our sincere gratitude to **Hassan Mohamed**, who has served as our Chapter Secretary since its early days.

Hassan has been part of the journey of building INCOSE MENA from an idea into a growing community of systems engineering professionals across the Middle East and North Africa.

His time, dedication, and contributions to establishing the Chapter and laying its foundations have meant a great deal to all of us.

**Thank you, Hassan, for believing in this vision, for your commitment, and for everything you have contributed along the way.** We are truly grateful to have shared this journey with you and wish you continued success in all that comes next.

**📢 Volunteer Opportunity | INCOSE MENA Chapter Secretary**

As Hassan steps down from his role, we are opening the position of **Chapter Secretary** to a new volunteer who would like to contribute to the next chapter of our journey.

This is an opportunity to join the Chapter's leadership team, support its activities, and help strengthen the systems engineering community across the MENA region.

If you are organised, collaborative, and passionate about making a difference in our community, we would love to hear from you.

📩 **Interested?** Contact us at [mena@incose.net](mailto:mena@incose.net) to express your interest and learn more about the role.

Together, we continue building on the foundations laid by those who helped make INCOSE MENA a reality.

## Website

**Thank You, Hassan Mohamed, and an Opportunity to Join INCOSE MENA's Leadership**

INCOSE MENA extends its heartfelt appreciation to **Hassan Mohamed**, our outgoing Chapter Secretary, for his dedication, time, and invaluable contributions to establishing and developing the Chapter.

As part of the founding leadership team, Hassan helped lay the groundwork for a community dedicated to advancing systems engineering across the Middle East and North Africa. His commitment, support, and willingness to contribute his time have been an important part of our journey.

Building a new professional chapter takes more than a shared vision. It takes people who believe in that vision and are willing to put in the work to make it a reality. Hassan has been one of those people.

On behalf of the INCOSE MENA leadership and community, **thank you, Hassan, for everything you have contributed.** We are grateful for the journey we have shared and wish you every success in your future endeavours.

### Chapter Secretary Volunteer Opportunity

As we look ahead, INCOSE MENA is inviting expressions of interest for the position of **Chapter Secretary**.

The Secretary plays an important role in supporting the Chapter's leadership, coordinating governance activities, maintaining organisational records, and ensuring effective communication and follow-up across Chapter initiatives.

This volunteer leadership opportunity offers the chance to contribute directly to the continued growth of INCOSE MENA and collaborate with professionals who share a passion for advancing systems engineering in the region.

**Interested in joining us?**

Please review the Chapter Secretary role description and send your expression of interest to [**mena@incose.net**](mailto:mena@incose.net).

We look forward to welcoming someone who will help us build upon the strong foundations established by our founding team.
