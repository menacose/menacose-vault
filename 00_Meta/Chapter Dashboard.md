# Chapter Dashboard

Internal view of core-team participation, activity engagement and current delivery. Figures refresh when the source records change. Use the period filter for attendance history; workload and upcoming activities always show the current position.

Open [[Work Overview]], [[Action Register]], [[Activity Register]] or [[MENACOSE People]] to update the sources.

## Keeping Figures Current

- **Board Meetings:** Tick the people present in each dated meeting note. An entirely unchecked list remains unrecorded. Updated copies of the same meeting count once.
- **Activities:** Keep one row per actual edition in [[Activity Register]]. Confirm Held after delivery, then add registrations and attendees from the event report. Leave unknown figures blank; enter zero only when confirmed.
- **Work:** Edit the connected meeting tables or the shared registers. Finish each meeting to keep its historical snapshot.

## Reading The Figures

Participation is the share of listed opportunities marked present, across meetings with attendance recorded. Unchecked names are not treated as confirmed absences. Former role holders are retained in the vault but excluded from the current core-team comparison.

Activity visits can include the same person at several editions. Attendance / Registrations uses only editions with both figures and a positive registration count; walk-ins can make it exceed 100%. Published announcements do not establish that an activity was held.

Contact notes are not a chapter membership census. Membership growth and unique participant counts remain unreported until suitable records exist.
