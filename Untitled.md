At INCOSE MENA, we believe that Working Groups (WGs) are the driving force for collaboration, innovation, and knowledge exchange across our community. They provide a focused platform where members can share expertise, address challenges, and advance systems engineering practices across various domains.

If you would like to propose a new Working Group, please follow the steps below:
**Proposal Requirements  
**Your proposal should include:

1.       Working Group Title: A clear and concise name that reflects the focus area.

2.       Objectives: The goals and intended outcomes of the Working Group (e.g., knowledge sharing, developing best practices, producing guidance).

3.       Leadership Team: At least one identified Leader/Chair, with the option to nominate Co-Leaders or deputies who will support in guiding the group.

**Submission & Review Process**

1-Submit your proposal directly to the Programs Director of INCOSE MENA.

2-The proposal will be reviewed by the Core Team to ensure alignment with INCOSE MENA’s vision and strategic priorities.

3-Final approval will be granted by the President of INCOSE MENA.

**Collaboration Opportunities**

1-Twin Programs with Other Chapters: Working Groups in MENA can establish partnerships and joint programs with Working Groups in other INCOSE Chapters worldwide.

2-Related Working Groups : Any global INCOSE Working Group can also initiate a related Working Group under INCOSE MENA to strengthen local engagement and regional contribution.

 **Joining an Existing Working Group**

If you are interested in contributing to an existing Working Group, you can join by simply reaching out to the Leader/Chair of that Working Group. They will guide you on how to participate in meetings, activities, and ongoing initiatives.

You can find the full list of current INCOSE MENA Working Groups on the official INCOSE MENA website here: INCOSE MENA Working Groups

**Next Steps After Approval**

Once approved, the new Working Group will be:

1-Officially announced on the INCOSE MENA communication channels.

2-Provided with support to recruit members, organize meetings, and plan activities.

3-Integrated into INCOSE MENA’s broader programs and initiatives.
