# Working Group Review Template

Use this one-page template to review each INCOSE MENA Working Group consistently. It helps leadership compare progress, identify challenges, and follow up on agreed actions.

## Word Template

[[90_Attachments/INCOSE_MENA_Working_Group_Review_Branded_Capitalized.docx|Open The Working Group Review Template In Word]]

## Review Areas

| Area | What To Include |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose & Focus | Working group objectives and current focus. |
| Progress | Activities, milestones, and achievements during the reporting period. |
| Outputs & Engagement | Deliverables, participation, and collaboration. |
| Challenges | Risks, blockers, and support required. |
| Next Priorities | Planned actions, owners, and deadlines. |
| Review Conclusion | Overall assessment and decisions. |

**Overall Status:** On Track / At Risk / Delayed.

## Suggested Use

1. Each working group lead saves and completes a separate copy before a monthly or quarterly review meeting. Include the group name and reporting period in the filename, and keep the original template for reuse.
2. Link the completed report from the working group's note and the review meeting note.
3. During the review, confirm the overall status, decisions, and support needed. Record agreed follow-up actions, owners, and deadlines in [[Action Register]], and confirm the next review date.
